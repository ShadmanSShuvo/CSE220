## CSE 220: Signals and Linear Systems (Sessional)

### General Information

* **Institution:** Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)
* **Department:** Department of Computer Science and Engineering
* **Credits:** 0.75 Credits (1.5 hours/week)
* **Semester:** January 2026
* **Textbook / Primary Reference:** *Signals and Systems* by Alan V. Oppenheim, Alan S. Willsky, with S. Hamid Nawab

---

### Course Objectives

1. Master continuous and discrete-time signals, linear systems, and their fundamental properties.
2. Formulate and compute Fourier Series, Continuous Fourier Transforms, Discrete/Fast Fourier Transforms, Laplace, and Z-transforms.
3. Apply computational transform methods in Python to real-world engineering problems (filtering, audio, vector graphics, image processing).
